# L'Ultimo Treno per Valdoria (The Last Train to Valdoria)

Hidden object game, real style, 1928. Four levels of four scenes.

## Story

Autumn 1928. Elena Ferri, a young telegraph operator in Milan, receives an unsigned
telegram: "The 21:40 to Valdoria still runs. Come before the snow. - A."
Her grandfather Augusto, station master of the alpine line to Valdoria, disappeared
ten years earlier on the night the last train left and never arrived; the line was
closed and the village emptied.

Elena follows the clues he left along the line: a ticket that should not exist, a
logbook with missing pages, a key hidden in a signal box. She learns that Augusto kept
the last train in the mountain to protect Valdoria's secret - the gold of the old
mine that the railway company wanted to seize - and that he is still waiting for
someone to bring the logbook back.

## Art direction (backgrounds)

- 1920s Italian alps, photorealistic, calm composition.
- **Uncluttered**: few large elements, clean open surfaces (tables, shelves, floor,
  benches) left free to host the hidden objects placed later in the editor.
- At most one small faded poster; no people; no readable text.
