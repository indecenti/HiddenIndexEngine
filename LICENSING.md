Plain-language summary. The binding texts are [LICENSE](LICENSE) (code),
[LICENSE-ASSETS.md](LICENSE-ASSETS.md) (assets) and

license text disagree, the license text wins.

## The short version

HiddenIndexEngine is **free for noncommercial use** and **paid for commercial use**.

| What you are doing | Terms |
|---|---|
| Learning, experimenting, a hobby game you give away for free | Free, under [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) |
| A school, university, public research body, charity, public institution | Free, under the same license |
| Selling a game, or giving it away with ads, in-app purchases, sponsorship or any other revenue | **Commercial license required** |
| Any use inside a company, agency or studio, including internal or unreleased projects | **Commercial license required** |
| Paid work for a client built with the engine | **Commercial license required** |
| Reselling, sublicensing or bundling the engine or the editor in your own product | **Commercial license required** |

If you make money with it, the author gets a share. That is the whole idea: the project
stays open to read, learn from and build on, and commercial value flows back to the person
who built it.

## Getting a commercial license

Open an issue titled **"Commercial license"** on
<https://github.com/indecenti/HiddenIndexEngine/issues>, or contact the author through
GitHub ([@indecenti](https://github.com/indecenti)).

Say what you plan to build, how it will be distributed and how it will make money. Terms
are agreed case by case — flat fee, revenue share or a per-title license, scaled to the
size of the project. Small independent projects are welcome; the point is not to lock the
door, it is to be paid when the engine earns money for someone else.

Nothing here is an offer or a binding commitment: a commercial license exists only once it
is signed in writing by the author.

## Assets

The images, music and sound effects bundled in the repository are **not** covered by the
code license. They are released under **CC BY-NC 4.0** (attribution, noncommercial) — see
[LICENSE-ASSETS.md](LICENSE-ASSETS.md). A commercial license for the code does not
automatically include them: say so when you ask, and they can be covered in the same
agreement.
